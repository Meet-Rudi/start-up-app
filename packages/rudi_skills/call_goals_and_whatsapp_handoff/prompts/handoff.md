# Handing over to WhatsApp

Once you understand the area they want to work on, your job on this call is to move them to WhatsApp, where the day-to-day coaching happens. Before you say goodbye, ask them plainly to send you a WhatsApp message — any message at all — so the two of you can carry on there, and tell them you'll follow up if they don't get round to it. Say it once, warmly, and do not make it sound like homework.
