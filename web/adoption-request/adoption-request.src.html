    Good questions

    Before you ask.

    What if I stop halfway?

    Your answers are saved after every step. Come back on the same device and you carry on where you left off. We have your contact details, so we may also get in touch to finish it together.

    Can our team step in when Rudi talks to people?

    Yes. Your team gets a cockpit to follow conversations, take one over, pause Rudi for someone or correct a reply. You tell us how closely you want to be involved.

    Do we need our own AI subscription?

    No. Rudi supplies and runs the AI, hosted in the EU, as part of the service. If you already have an AI contract you’d like to use, you can tell us in the last step.

    Does Rudi give medical advice?

    No. Rudi helps people follow the plan they got from you. Questions about diagnosis, medication or treatment go back to you or their clinician.

    Where is the data kept?

    In the EU, under a data processing agreement with your organisation. Your organisation’s data is kept apart from every other organisation’s.

    What does a pilot cost?

    We agree it with you when we shape the pilot, based on the number of people and channels.
