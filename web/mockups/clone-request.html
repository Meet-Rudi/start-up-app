Privacy & trust

Personal support. Privacy built in.

  • Rudi’s AI runs on EU-hosted infrastructure.
  • Your organisation’s data is kept apart from every other organisation’s.
  • A data processing agreement before anyone talks to Rudi.
  • No medical advice: Rudi points people back to you.

Good questions

Before you ask.

Does Rudi give medical advice?

No. Rudi helps people follow the plan they got from you. Questions about diagnosis, medication or treatment go back to you or their clinician.

Where is the data kept?

In the EU, under a data processing agreement with your organisation. Your organisation’s data is kept apart from every other organisation’s.

Can Rudi use our own WhatsApp number?

Yes. It takes a little longer to set up than a Rudi number, so many pilots start on a Rudi number.

What does a pilot cost?

We agree it with you when we shape the pilot, based on the number of people and channels.
